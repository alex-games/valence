HOW TO PLAY

The beginner level

You spawn on the plaza at the centre of a 96 × 96 block Torrance. The Community Garden is north-west, Lago Seco Park is north-east, The Coffee Bean is south-east and Martin Chevrolet is south-west. Follow the pale paths and the floating place names.

Quest list. Visit the four landmarks, place ten blocks and break five. Everything is safe: water lets you swim, flying lets you build anywhere, and nothing can hurt you.
